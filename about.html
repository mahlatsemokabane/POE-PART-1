Meet Brew & Bean

Brew & Bean Artisan Coffee was founded in 2018 by siblings Mia and James Peterson. They began with a small coffee cart at local farmers’ markets, serving ethically sourced, single-origin coffee and homemade pastries.

In 2020 they opened their first brick-and-mortar café, which has become a neighbourhood gathering spot. See what is on at our events page.

Serve exceptional, ethically sourced coffee while creating a welcoming space where community connections are brewed daily.

Become the most loved local coffee shop chain in the region, known for quality, sustainability and community engagement.

Ethical sourcing, sustainability, a warm atmosphere and community involvement guide every cup we serve.
